SUFFIX DONATES SPORTS EQUIPMENT TO BOOST SCHOOL SPORTS IN CHITIPA

Chitipa District Education Sports Officer Patrick Simwayi has commended musician Aubrey Ghambi, popularly known as Suffix, for donating sports equipment to Muswero and Chinunkha Primary Schools.

Simwayi said the district faces many challenges in providing sports materials to all schools, adding that the donation has helped ease some of the difficulties. He noted that sports and education go hand in hand, as they help keep learners active and engaged in school.

Primary Education Advisor for Ifumbo Zone, Kisa Masebo, also appreciated the donation and urged other well-wishers to support schools with similar initiatives.

Representing Suffix, Michael Ngambi said the initiative, coordinated with support from Chitipa Platform, aims to encourage pupils to participate in sports while promoting teamwork and discipline.

LUVURI FC CROWNED CHAMPIONS OF THE NRFA DIVISION

Luvuri FC have been crowned champions of the NRFA Division One League after defeating Moyale Reserves 8–7 on penalties following a 3–3 draw in the final played at Luwinga Inclusive Academy Ground in Mzuzu.

Chisomo Magombo scored twice for Moyale in the 54th and 59th minutes, while Waliko Mhango added another in the 46th minute. Luvuri responded through Lucky Mkandawire, who netted a hat-trick in the 51st, 77th and 90+5th minutes.

Chamaliwa FC claimed the bronze medal after edging Luwerezi Pirates 4-3.

NRFA Vice Chairperson Epimack Lwanja described the tournament as exciting and commended teams and supporters for maintaining peace throughout the competition.

EMBANGWENI UNITED CROWNED CHAMPIONS OF M’MBELWA NORTHERN REGION FOOTBALL LEAGUE

The Northern Region Football Association (NRFA) says it is impressed with the level of competition in the M’mbelwa Northern Region Football League following the conclusion of the season on Sunday.

The final match saw Ekwaiweni Tafika Sports Academy draw 1-1 with Embangweni United at Ekwaiweni Ground.

Speaking during the symbolic trophy presentation to crown Embangweni United as champions, NRFA chairperson Masiya Nyasulu said the association believes the team has what it takes to compete in the National Division League.

Coach Dan Lusale said his team is ready for the National Division challenge despite concerns about limited rest for players before the new season.

As champions, Embangweni United have been promoted to the National Division League and will receive K7 million, while runners-up Lube Masters will get K4 million.
