Emmie Deebo Sets Balaka Ablaze with Electrifying Live Performance

Balaka was alive with music and excitement two days ago as Malawian female artist Emmie Deebo delivered a powerful and unforgettable live performance that left fans chanting for more.

Taking to the stage with confidence and infectious energy, Emmie Deebo quickly connected with the crowd, blending soulful vocals with energetic dance moves. From the opening song, fans surged closer to the stage, singing along and waving their hands in the air as the artist showcased why she remains one of Malawi’s most captivating performers.

Her performance featured a mix of her popular songs, including tracks that highlight themes of love, resilience, and everyday life experiences that resonate deeply with her audience. The crowd responded with loud cheers, especially during her emotional delivery of slower songs, where many fans could be seen recording the moment on their phones.

Speaking briefly on stage, Emmie Deebo thanked her supporters in Balaka for their continued love, describing the reception as “overwhelming and motivating.” She encouraged young people, especially girls, to believe in their dreams and remain focused despite challenges.

The event attracted music lovers from different parts of Balaka and surrounding areas, further proving Emmie Deebo’s growing influence in Malawi’s music industry. Local organizers praised her professionalism and stage presence, noting that her performance brought a unique spark to the event.

As the show came to an end, fans were left buzzing with excitement, many describing the performance as one of the best live shows Balaka has hosted in recent months. Emmie Deebo’s stage appearance not only entertained but also reinforced her status as a strong voice and performer in Malawian music.

ONESIMUS DELIVERS UNFORGETTABLE SHOW AT BICC

The atmosphere at the Bingu International Convention Centre (BICC) in Lilongwe was electric last night as fans gathered in large numbers for a highly anticipated performance by Onesimus.

Dressed in a stylish stage outfit and backed by a vibrant live band, Onesimus stepped onto the stage to deafening cheers from the crowd. From the very first note, it was clear that the audience was in for an unforgettable night.

The award-winning artist delivered a powerful set filled with his popular hits, blending Afro-pop rhythms with energetic dance moves that kept fans on their feet.

The show, which attracted music lovers from across Lilongwe and beyond, was marked by bright stage lights, synchronized dancers, and a well-coordinated sound system that amplified every beat. At several points during the performance, fans sang along word for word, turning the venue into a chorus of excitement and unity.

One of the highlights of the night came when Onesimus paused to thank his supporters for their continued love and loyalty. He spoke about the growth of Malawian music and encouraged upcoming artists to remain consistent and focused. The crowd responded with applause and chants of his name.

As the night progressed, the energy only intensified. The artist moved across the stage with confidence, interacting with fans at the front row and creating a personal connection that made the event feel intimate despite the large turnout.

By the end of the concert, it was clear that the show had lived up to expectations. Fans left the venue smiling, some describing the event as one of the best live performances in recent months. Social media was quickly flooded with photos and videos from the night, with many praising Onesimus for delivering a world-class performance.

The concert not only entertained but also reinforced Onesimus’ position as one of Malawi’s leading contemporary performers.

MAYAMIKO CHIWAULA CROWNED MISS CULTURE MALAWI 2026

Excitement and celebration filled the atmosphere on 28 February 2026 as Mayamiko Chiwaula was crowned Miss Culture Malawi 2026 in a colorful ceremony that celebrated the country’s rich heritage and traditions.

The event, attracted cultural enthusiasts, supporters, and members of the entertainment industry who gathered to witness the crowning of a new ambassador of Malawian culture. Contestants showcased different aspects of Malawi’s traditions through cultural attire, dance performances and poetry.

Chiwaula stood out among the finalists with her confidence, stage presence and articulate responses during the question-and-answer segment. When asked about the role of young people in preserving culture, she responded: “As young people, we must not see culture as something old-fashioned. Our traditions define who we are. If we neglect them, we lose our identity as Malawians.”

Her response drew loud applause from the audience and appeared to impress the judges.

Upon receiving the crown, an emotional Chiwaula thanked her supporters and pledged to use her title responsibly. “This crown is not just for me, but for every young girl who believes in embracing her roots. I promise to promote our culture with pride and passion,” she said.

One of the event organizers described the competition as more than just a beauty pageant. “Miss Culture Malawi is about promoting heritage, values and unity. We are looking for someone who understands and appreciates the depth of our traditions,” the organizer said.

The night was filled with vibrant traditional dances, cheers from supporters and ululations as the crown was officially placed on Chiwaula’s head.

With the title now officially hers, Mayamiko Chiwaula begins her reign as Miss Culture Malawi 2026 with a mission to champion cultural awareness among Malawi’s youth.
